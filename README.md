# USI–UBS Field Project

This field project at Università della Svizzera italiana (USI), carried out with UBS, explores Canton Ticino’s attractiveness as a destination for High Net Worth Individuals (HNWIs) and Ultra High Net Worth Individuals (UHNWIs) through comparisons with other destinations and stakeholder interviews.

Data, sources and findings will be added progressively after the team has collected and verified them.

## Research structure

The [shared research structure](02_methodology/benchmark_methodology.md#comparison-dimensions) covers:

- Wealth
- Banking presence and density
- Business presence
- Employment
- Stability and institutions
- Quality of life
- Taxation and relocation
- Real estate
- Population and migration
- Healthcare
- Education
