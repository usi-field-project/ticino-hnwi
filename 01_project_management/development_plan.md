# Development plan

Draft for team review. Dates and the detailed methodology remain to be agreed.

## Project objectives

Assess Ticino’s attractiveness for high-net-worth individuals (HNWIs) relative to the selected Swiss and international destinations, and identify evidence-based opportunities for UBS in Ticino.

## Scope and deliverables

Produce destination research sheets, a comparative benchmark, a synthesis of interviews, and a final report and presentation with recommendations for UBS. Use Ticino as the baseline and the [shared research topics](../README.md#shared-research-topics) as the collection grid.

## Work packages

1. **Agree on the framework.** Define the target client segments, research questions and comparable indicators before collecting data in depth.
2. **Research each destination.** Follow the [assignments and research sheets](../README.md#research-by-destination). Each researcher records findings, sources, reference periods and geographical coverage, reusing existing materials where relevant.
3. **Build the comparison.** Consolidate findings into a common benchmark, check comparability and identify gaps. Agree on any scoring method before calculating rankings.
4. **Add interview insights.** Prepare and conduct interviews to explore client priorities and practical relocation considerations, then summarise the findings anonymously alongside the desk research.
5. **Develop recommendations.** Identify Ticino’s strengths and weaknesses by client segment, assess implications for UBS, and prepare the final report and presentation.

## Review and acceptance criteria

Review the consolidated benchmark together before drawing conclusions. Key claims must be traceable to sources; differences in dates, geographical scope and definitions must be explicit. Record missing evidence and uncertainty. Recommendations should follow from the findings and distinguish supported conclusions from hypotheses requiring further validation.
