# Lugano Airport (LUG / LSZA) – business aviation access for Ticino

Collected by: Elena · Access date: 22.10.2026 · Status: `to_verify`

Recorded in the data register as `TI-035` (`business_aviation_airport`). See [`03_data/observations.csv`](../../../../../../03_data/observations.csv).

## Key findings

| Topic | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Agno, about 6 km by road from Lugano city centre (4 km in a straight line); about 80 km from Milan | Wikipedia |
| Ownership and management | Land owned by the City of Lugano; managed directly by the City since Lugano Airport SA was liquidated in 2020 | Wikipedia, RSI |
| Federal concession | Renewed by DETEC for 30 years, **until 2056** (September 2026) | RSI |
| Scheduled flights | None since 2019 (Adria Airways, operating Zurich–Lugano for Swiss, stopped; Swiss moved passengers to the train). No regular flights as of March 2025 | Wikipedia, RSI |
| Current traffic | Business aviation, Rega and institutional flights have grown since 2019 | RSI |
| Runway | Single runway 01/19, 1,420 m × 30 m, asphalt, 279 m elevation | Wikipedia (Swiss AIP) |
| Approach | Instrument approach to RWY 01 at **6.65°** (standard is 3°; London City 5.5°) | Wikipedia |
| Aircraft admitted | Only jets certified for steep approach can fly the RWY 01 approach; examples: Gulfstream G280, G500 and G600 have demonstrated landings at Lugano | General Dynamics, Airframer |
| Possible scheduled service | The Council of States approved foreign airlines operating domestic Swiss flights (cabotage); still needs the National Council. Could make Lugano–Geneva viable again | RSI |

## Implications for the benchmark

- **Strength for UHNWIs:** a business-aviation airport about 6 km from the reference municipality (Lugano), with a concession secured until 2056.
- **Limits:** access depends on the type of jet (steep-approach certification) and on the weather. In bad weather flights divert to Milan Malpensa.
- **Scheduled and long-haul flights:** Ticino depends on Malpensa (`TI-028`) and Zurich. Lugano Airport does not replace a hub.
